# Chakshi.com — One-Page Executive Brief
**Responsible, Tamil-first legal AI — built in Tamil Nadu, already live.**

**Founder:** Jaiy Chandhar Chandrasekaran, Advocate, Coimbatore · jaiychandhar.advocate@gmail.com · https://www.chakshi.com

---

**What it is.** A matter-first legal *operating system* for advocates, law offices, clerks, juniors, students and citizens. An advocate uploads a case bundle; Chakshi returns a case brief, timeline, issue list, evidence chart, contradiction list, missing-document list, limitation warning and first draft — each item traceable to its source page. The advocate remains the final decision-maker.

**Why now / why it's credible.** It is **already in production.** The live engine performs compliant eCourts case intelligence — search by CNR and party name; case status, orders, cause lists, caveats; structured extraction — with **human-solved CAPTCHA** (no bypass) and resilient, maintained scrapers. The hardest question, *can the team ship?*, is already answered.

**The safety design.** *Verify or classify.* Every AI output shows source, page, quote, confidence, missing-info warnings, and an explicit "not found in record" — never confident-but-wrong. India-resident data, DPDP-2023-aligned, audit-logged. Positioned strictly as software: no legal advice, no client solicitation, no advocate referral — fully consistent with Bar Council rules.

**Why Tamil Nadu wins.** First-mover State on responsible legal AI; a **Tamil-first** public legal-literacy layer; district-level digitisation beyond the metros (Coimbatore, Chennai, Madurai, Salem, Tiruppur, Erode); skilled TN-based jobs in AI, engineering and legal ops; a TN-origin SaaS with national scale and the State as founding partner.

**The ask.** **₹4 crore over 18 months, milestone-linked** — grant / seed fund / matching, the Department's preferred instrument. Path to self-sufficiency via tiered SaaS (free tiers for juniors/students) keeps the public legal-literacy layer free as a public good — a one-time catalyst, not a recurring cost.
